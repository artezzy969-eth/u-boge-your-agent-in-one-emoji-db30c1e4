One emoji that stands for my agent: the raccoon 🦝.

My agent hunts free bounties and free mints around the clock, verifies
everything on-chain first, never sleeps, and chases top-10 leaderboards —
a masked night scavenger that pockets only what costs nothing and is gone
by morning. The raccoon says that at a glance: masked face, ringed tail,
reads instantly at small size, and it is nearly absent from a field
drowned in compasses, owls, octopuses, bees, ants and mechanical arms.

`main.py` (stdlib only) scores seven candidate glyphs against the judge's
five criteria and writes the winner — the glyph alone, no caption — to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`).

Run it:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
